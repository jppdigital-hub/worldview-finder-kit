# Worldview Finder version history

## 1.0.1

- Wording update, approved by Paul. Five Life Story questions now say "memory" (or "time" in one case) instead of "moment". Four follow-ups lost notes meant for the AI or became clearer. The page, README and templates were rewritten in plainer words, and AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md were added for coding agents.

## 1.0.0

First public version: the interview prompt, 22 Life Story questions, 68 Worldview questions, record templates, a short fictional example, and setup steps for ChatGPT and Claude Projects.

Dan P. McAdams's *The Life Story Interview II* from the Foley Center for the Study of Lives at Northwestern University influenced the Life Story structure. This kit uses original question wording. [Life Story Interview II](https://cpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.northwestern.edu/dist/4/3901/files/2020/11/The-Life-Story-Interview-II-2007.pdf)
