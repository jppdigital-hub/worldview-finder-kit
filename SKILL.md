# Worldview Finder

Read [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md) in full and follow it for the interview, record, and optional outputs. Use the bundled [Life Story questions](LIFE-STORY-QUESTIONS.md) and [Worldview questions](WORLDVIEW-QUESTIONS.md). Read the [index template](INDEX-TEMPLATE.md) and [archive template](ARCHIVE-TEMPLATE.md) when preparing or resuming a record.

Use only material the participant provides or approves for this interview. Keep each interview turn to one question, preserve their exact words, and label every AI interpretation. Use files only at a destination they choose and authorize. Keep all records and drafts private by default.
